## 1.1. 受核内容

| 词段 | 短释 | 公开事实来源 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| neural tissue | 神经组织 | NCI 的 [neural](https://www.cancer.gov/publications/dictionaries/cancer-terms/def/neural) 与 [tissue](https://www.cancer.gov/publications/dictionaries/cancer-terms/def/tissue) 给出组合含义。 |
| fatty tissue | 脂肪组织 | NCI 的 [fatty breast tissue](https://www.cancer.gov/publications/dictionaries/cancer-terms/def/fatty-breast-tissue) 与 SEER 的 [connective tissue](https://training.seer.cancer.gov/anatomy/cells_tissues_membranes/tissues/connective.html) 支持限定含义。 |

| spinal cord | 脊髓 | NCI 的 [spinal cord](https://www.cancer.gov/publications/dictionaries/cancer-terms/def/spinal-cord) 给出解剖含义。 |
| nerve fiber | 神经纤维 | [NCI SEER](https://training.seer.cancer.gov/anatomy/nervous/tissue.html) 与 [NLM MeSH](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/mesh/68009412) 支持此解剖词段。 |
| fatty substance | 脂肪性物质 | [NCI SEER](https://training.seer.cancer.gov/anatomy/nervous/tissue.html) 提供单数事实；复数的独立依据限于 [Oxford 官方页面](https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/definition/english/substance) 的索引，不视为直读原页。此词段不等于髓鞘。 |

每条仅覆盖目录中明确列出的单数和复数，不推导其他词形、不声称频率或普遍语境消歧。短释由项目整理，不复刻第三方释义或例句。
